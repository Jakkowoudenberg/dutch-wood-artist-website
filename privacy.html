Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Dutch Wood Artist · KvK 51624842

This privacy policy explains how Dutch Wood Artist (operated by Jakko Woudenberg, registered in the Netherlands under KvK number 51624842, based in Schagen) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use this website.

1. Who we are

Dutch Wood Artist is a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) registered in the Netherlands. The owner and data controller is Jakko Woudenberg, reachable through the options on the contact page.

2. What data we collect

You can contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email. We receive the contact details and message you choose to share through that service. This website no longer has a contact form or AI chat.

When your browser requests a page or a font, the hosting and font providers receive technical information needed to deliver it, such as your IP address. No advertising or analytics scripts are installed on this site.

3. Why we collect your data

We use your contact details to respond to your inquiry. Your data is not used for marketing without your consent or sold to third parties. The communication service you choose processes your message as part of delivering it.

4. How long we keep your data

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and for up to two years thereafter for administrative purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Where your data is stored

The website is hosted by Netlify. New inquiries go through your chosen phone, WhatsApp or email service rather than a website form. Messages sent using the former contact form remain in our Netlify account subject to the retention period above.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. For information about these providers, see Netlify's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion of your data, object to processing, and request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact page. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

7. Cookies

The current site does not use Google Analytics or set advertising or analytics cookies. External websites and communication services have their own cookie and privacy policies.

8. Third-party links

This website contains links to external websites including thenightwatchinwood.com, app.unitedwoodfloorlayers.com, archdaily.com and others. Dutch Wood Artist is not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or your personal data, use the options on the contact page.
